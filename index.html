How an engagement runs

  1. A 30-minute call

    You tell us what you're trying to build or fix and what it runs on. We tell you if it's a fit and what it would roughly take.

  2. A fixed-price proposal

    Within a few days: scope, price, and a delivery date in writing. No hourly meter unless you prefer one.

  3. Build in your codebase, demo every week

    Work happens in your repo and your accounts. You see working software weekly, not a slide deck at the end.

  4. Handover and a month of support

    Tests, documentation, and a walkthrough for your team. We stay on for 30 days after launch for anything that comes up.

The terms, up front

  • You own the code and everything we produce.
  • NDA signed before we look at anything.
  • Your data stays in your systems and never trains a model.
  • The engineer you talk to is the one who does the work. No handoff to a junior team.
  • Pay by milestone, with the last payment after it's live.

How long does it take?

A connector or feature: two to four weeks. A new app or a rebuild: six to twelve weeks, delivered screen by screen so you're never waiting on a big bang.

What do you need from us?

Access to the repo and a test environment, one person who can answer questions about the system, and an hour a week for the demo.

Which AI models do you use?

The one that fits the job and your data rules. We work with the major commercial models through their APIs, and we tell you which one we would use and why before anything starts.

What happens after launch?

Thirty days of support are included. After that, a monthly retainer if you want us on call, or nothing. The code is yours and documented either way.
